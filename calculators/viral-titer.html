How these are calculated

Reed–Muench: positives are accumulated from the highest dilution downward and negatives from the lowest dilution upward; the 50 % point is interpolated between the two dilutions that bracket it (proportionate distance). Spearman–Kärber: log₁₀ end-point = x₀ + d·(S − 0.5), where x₀ is the highest dilution with 100 % positive wells, d = log₁₀(dilution factor), and S = sum of the proportions positive from x₀ onward. The 95 % interval uses SE = d·√(Σp(1−p)/(n−1)). TCID₅₀/mL = end-point titer ÷ inoculum volume in mL. The PFU conversion (×0.69) is the usual Poisson approximation, not an exact equivalence.
