Interpretation guide
ISRResultMeaning
< 0.90NegativeNo detectable IgM to Chikungunya virus. Does not rule out acute infection if collected in the first 1–5 days after symptom onset (before IgM rises) — consider RT-PCR, or retest a convalescent sample after 7–14 days.
0.90 – 1.10EquivocalIndeterminate. Retest in duplicate. If still equivocal, collect a fresh serum 7–14 days later to look for seroconversion, or use a confirmatory method (e.g. PRNT).
> 1.10PositivePresumptive positive for anti-CHIKV IgM — recent or acute infection. IgM can persist for months and may cross-react with other alphaviruses (e.g. Mayaro, O'nyong-nyong, Ross River): correlate with symptoms and travel/exposure history.

Run validity: mean NC ≤ 0.200 · mean PC ≥ 0.500 · mean CC > mean NC · PC/NC (discrimination capacity, RPC/NC) ≥ 5.0. ISR = sample OD₄₅₀ ÷ mean CC OD₄₅₀. ISR and PC/NC are compared as displayed, to 2 decimals.
